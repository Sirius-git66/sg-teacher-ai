What you are buying

A labelled slot among the commercial tool entries on this site. The site is visited by Singapore teachers who need to know which AI tools they can use under MOE rules. Your tool appears alongside the tools they are comparing you against.

You are not buying a verdict. Your tool stays rated on its merits. If your verdict is Conditional, it stays Conditional. If you provide verifiable data-retention terms, we can re-evaluate — but the rating is never for sale.

Three rules enforced in code, not promises

  1. Official MOE tools are never for sale. The build fails if a sponsor is configured next to an official tool. Selling a slot beside something MOE built would destroy the only thing this site has.
  2. Sponsorship does not buy a verdict. A sponsored tool stays marked. The mark stays when a verdict drops to a warning. A vendor can correct our facts; they cannot buy a "Permitted" rating.
  3. Commercial tools can never be rated "Permitted." We cannot audit a vendor's data handling, so the ceiling is "Conditional" and the build fails if anyone tries to exceed it.

Why this is worth considering

MOE is rolling out a Teacher AI Literacy Professional Learning Roadmap, with a first compulsory module due end of 2026 for all teachers. Teachers are about to be mandatory-trained on AI. This site is where they check what is allowed.

A vendor listed here with transparent conditions is positioned as compliant and trustworthy. A vendor not listed at all — or listed as Hold with no response — is the one a teacher remembers when she is filling out her school's AI risk assessment.
